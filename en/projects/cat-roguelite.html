About the project

A roguelite with a clan life simulation, inspired by the Warriors book series and ClanGen. The player controls a single cat born into a clan. They explore a tile-based world with fog of war, build relationships with NPCs, and after death receive a procedurally generated epitaph. Developed in Godot 4 / GDScript as a personal project.

Stack

  • Engine and language: Godot 4.6, GDScript
  • Art: pixel art in Aseprite, layered cat sprites (body, eyes, shadow) for each life stage, palette_swap and shadow shaders
  • Data: content stored in JSON (appearance, names, conditions/injuries, loot, causes of death, traits)
  • Tools: Git, project documentation in Obsidian

Architecture

  • Separation of data, logic and presentation. Data lives in Resource classes: CatData, RelationshipData, WorldEvent, LifeLogEntry, MapTile. Logic lives in singleton systems (autoloads): GameState, CatManager, KinshipSystem, TimeskipSystem, PlayerKnowledgeSystem, CatPlacementSystem, ClanGenerator, MapGenerator. Scenes only display state.
  • Relationship graph. A directed dictionary {from_id → {to_id → RelationshipData}}. Bonds (parent/child, mate, mentor, rival, CO_PARENT and more) are stored as a set of bond types. Their semantics are deliberately kept apart: for example, CO_PARENT (“we had kittens together”) is separate from MATE, laying the groundwork for a scandal mechanic.
  • KinshipSystem. A read-only query layer over kinship: parents, siblings, grandparents, cousins, mates and former mates. All functions are typed and work by id.
  • Signals. The cat manager emits events (cat_died, cat_born, cat_promoted, etc.) without knowing anything about its listeners. This decouples logic, the life log and the UI.
  • Event model (hybrid). Active events are tied to tiles and player actions. Passive ones (births, deaths, injuries during a timeskip) obey the fog of war: the player learns about them through dialogue rather than by “seeing” them. The shared event engine (EventResolver) is built on a registry of named predicates and JSON event descriptions.

Highlights

  • A full clan life cycle: aging, roles and promotions, mentorship, pregnancy, birth, death. The related bonds automatically become “former” ones (MATE → PAST_MATE, MENTOR → PAST_MENTOR).
  • A condition system (illnesses, injuries, pregnancy) processed monthly, with possible loss of limbs.
  • Generation of “phantom” dead parents so every cat has a complete family tree.
  • Knowledge-level fog of war: “heard of a cat” and “has seen it” are different states, and dead cats don't count as seen.
  • Clan and tile map generation with activities (hunting, herb gathering, digging) and NPC placement across the territory.
  • Procedural epitaphs based on the cat's life log.
  • Retro look: 1152×864 resolution (4:3), 96 px tiles, pixel font.

Inspiration

The Warriors book series and the browser game ClanGen. The main difference from ClanGen: instead of abstract text patrols, there's a real map that the player walks around and lives in.
