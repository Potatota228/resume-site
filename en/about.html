About me

Behind the facts on a resume there's always a person, so let me tell you a bit more about that person :]

I run and play D&D (the well-known tabletop role-playing game). I love funny, slightly absurd stories, and I take my characters unreasonably seriously and write them in unreasonable detail. For me, D&D is a way to create a game and play it at the same time, in good company. I also write short stories, mostly in English — it keeps my language skills sharp and gives an outlet to the endless plots brewing in my head. I'm into birdwatching, too — you may have noticed it in a few of my portfolio projects. Birds are wonderful, and because of them I often roam the nearby woods with a camera in hand.

Game design is a story of its own. I was thrilled when I realized this profession exists, because it combines everything I am and everything I want to be. To me, a game is like an amusement ride built for a specific type of player and for the specific emotions it should evoke in them. And if the game feels like a ride the player doesn't want to get off, the game designer did a good job. As an aspiring game designer, I love taking other people's “rides” apart: looking at why a particular mechanic works the way it does, what it gives the player on its own, and what happens when it starts interacting with other mechanics in the system. That feeling when separate gears click together into one working machine is my greatest reward.
